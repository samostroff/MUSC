MUSC Sensory Room: Sonic Interventions

The MUSC Sensory Room offers a selection of sound-based experiences for your child. Based on your child's preferences, you can choose to engage in nature sounds, soothing music, or a series of interactive rhythm activities.

Setup

Before beginning the activities, please set the speaker volume using the iPad. The voice should sound at conversation volume.
