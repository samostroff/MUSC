MUSC Sensory Room: Sonic Interventions

The MUSC Sensory Room offers a selection of sound-based experiences for your child. Based on your child's preferences, you can choose immersive nature sounds, soothing music, or engage them in a series of interactive rhythm activities.

Player 1: Setup

Before beginning the activities, please set the speaker volume using the iPad. The voice should sound at conversation volume.

    Player 2: Natural Soundscapes

    These recordings of natural soundscapes include running water, birdsong, and forest ambiences.

      Player 3: Soothing Music

      These soothing music tracks were composed by professional musicians specifically for listeners on the autism spectrum.

        Player 4: Rhythm Activities

        These rhythm activities were created by professional musicians and medical doctors for use by people on the autism spectrum. Please follow along using your hands or the provided instrument(s).
